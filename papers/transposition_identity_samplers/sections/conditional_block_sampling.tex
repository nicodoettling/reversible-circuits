\section{Conditional Block Sampling}

For a block product $h\in S_N$, define
\[
  F_r(h) = |\mathcal{B}_r(h)|
  =
  |\{(s_1,\ldots,s_r)\in T_N^r : s_1\cdots s_r=h\}|.
\]
Let $M=|T_N|=\binom{N}{2}$.

\subsection{Prefix Rejection}

The simplest exact sampler for $\mathcal{B}_r(h)$ samples the first $r-1$
transpositions uniformly, computes the unique final factor needed to make the
product equal to $h$, and accepts iff that final factor is a transposition.
Every valid block has a unique prefix, so the accepted distribution is exactly
uniform on $\mathcal{B}_r(h)$.

\begin{lemma}[Prefix-rejection acceptance]
The acceptance probability of prefix rejection for block size $r$ and target
product $h$ is
\[
  A_r(h) = \frac{F_r(h)}{M^{r-1}}.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
There are $M^{r-1}$ possible prefixes.  Exactly $F_r(h)$ of them are prefixes
of valid length-$r$ factorizations of $h$ by transpositions.
\end{proof}

Since $T_N$ is a conjugacy class, $F_r(h)$ depends only on the cycle type of
$h$.  If $d(h)=N-c(h)$ is the transposition distance, where $c(h)$ is the
number of cycles including fixed points, then the standard parity and distance
constraints imply
\[
  F_r(h)=0
  \quad\text{unless}\quad
  d(h)\leq r
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  r-d(h)\equiv 0 \pmod 2.
\]
The previous run also found, with high confidence for exact counts and medium
confidence for an interpolated formula table, that for fixed $r$,
\[
  A_r(h)=O_r\left(N^{2-r-d(h)}\right).
\]
This asymptotic should be treated as a checked empirical/formula-guided claim
until a symbolic derivation is written.

Actual MCMC instrumentation confirmed the bottleneck.  For $r=4$ prefix
rejection, mean attempts were about $243.6$ at $N=8,m=20$, $1334$ at
$N=10,m=20$, $4809$ at $N=12,m=20$, and $5282$ at $N=12,m=30$.  The largest
observed attempt count in that run was $100388$.


\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Direct Exact Samplers for $r=4$ and $r=5$}{Direct Exact Samplers for r=4 and r=5}}

The direct conditional-sampler run completed the implementation target from the
previous version of this note.  It used the convention that permutations are
stored as tuples $p[x]$, multiplication means composition ``$p$ after $q$'',
and words are evaluated left to right by right-multiplying transpositions.  In
this convention, after choosing first transposition $t$ in a block with target
product $h$, the residual target is $th$.

For $r=4$, Worker A implemented an exact two-pair midpoint sampler.  Let
\[
  P_2(g)=\{(a,b)\in T_N^2 : ab=g\}.
\]
For target product $h$, sample a midpoint product $p$ with probability
proportional to
\[
  |P_2(p)|\,|P_2(p^{-1}h)|,
\]
then sample uniformly from $P_2(p)$ and $P_2(p^{-1}h)$.  Each valid block
$(t_1,t_2,t_3,t_4)$ has the unique midpoint $p=t_1t_2$, hence every valid
block is produced with probability $1/F_4(h)$.

For $r=5$, Worker B implemented exact completion-count recursion.  Let
$F_\ell(g)$ be the number of length-$\ell$ transposition words with product
$g$.  At residual target $g$, the next transposition $t$ is chosen with
probability proportional to $F_{\ell-1}(tg)$ in the project convention.  The
conditional probabilities telescope, so every valid length-$5$ block with
product $h$ has probability $1/F_5(h)$.

At $N=20$, the $r=4$ midpoint sampler peaked near $10.9$ MB during pair-fiber
construction, had worst measured target-kernel build time $21.3$ ms, and had
worst measured sampling time about $1.27$ microseconds per sample.  For a
6-cycle target at $N=20$, direct $r=5$ sampling took about $1.59$ ms per sample,
whereas prefix rejection was estimated at about $3.47$ seconds per sample, a
slowdown of roughly $2183\times$.  Thus the practical prefix-rejection
bottleneck for $r=4,5$ has been removed for the all-transpositions model.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{Integrated $\{3,4,5\}$ Heat-Bath Kernel}{Integrated {3,4,5} Heat-Bath Kernel}}

The promoted kernel chooses a block size from $\{3,4,5\}$, chooses a contiguous
block, computes its product, and replaces it by an exactly uniform block of the
same length and product.  Exact finite transition checks passed the standard
small cases.

The proposed implementation interface is
\[
\texttt{DirectTranspositionBlockSampler.count/sample\_block}
\]
together with a \texttt{block\_heat\_bath\_step} routine.  These primitives are
ready to promote for the abstract model $S_N$ with all transpositions.  They do
not automatically transfer to restricted reversible gate libraries, where the
same-product completion counts change.
