\section{Exact Small-Case Evidence}

The first exact harness enumerated all transposition words for three smoke
cases and retained the identity words.  It also built the Hurwitz graph and
recorded diagnostics such as prefix cycle types, edge frequencies, adjacent
repeat counts, support shapes, and window product types.

The first sampler-candidate run promoted both block heat-bath and
square-refresh-plus-Hurwitz on small cases: both preserved identity, visited
all exact Hurwitz components in the tested cases, and had acceptable empirical
diagnostic distances after finite runs.  The more important result is that the
exact move graphs for block heat-bath were connected in
$S_3,m=4$, $S_4,m=4$, and $S_4,m=6$.
