## Required analytic candidate

After source-locking and reconstructing the combined token and `c_X`
normalization, freeze one deterministic or finitely randomized source surgery
`S` and one source-local predicate `Exc`.  Both must be defined only from
marked-owner/head/seam geometry.  They may not depend on final profile-edge

The intended off-exception statement is:

1. `S` preserves the unordered raw-profile edge;
2. `S` reverses its orientation;
3. the two literal source weights satisfy an exact coboundary identity before
   any norm is taken;

   `C*d*b_d/k` for `H_d/A_d` and
   `C*max(d,e)*b_d*b_e/k` for `C_de`.

If no natural source-local surgery can meet item 1, stop at the first exact
local obstruction or counterfamily and state a strictly narrower repaired
lemma.  Do not decompose the target into separate channels, types, or cells.
