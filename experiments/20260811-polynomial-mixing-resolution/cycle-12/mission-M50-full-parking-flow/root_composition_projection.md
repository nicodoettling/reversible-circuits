# Exact full-Pbar root-composition projection and its polynomial gap

Date: 2026-08-11.  Permutations compose as `(pq)(x)=p(q(x))`, and
factor tuples multiply left-to-right.  This theorem concerns the full
nonlazy adjacent rank-two projection `Pbar_r` in the minimal factorization
sector of `(0 1 ... r)`.  It is not a theorem for the original identity
chain `K`.

## 1. Statement

For `r>=2`, let

```text
J_r={d in N^r:sum_i d_i=r and sum_(i=0)^q d_i>=q+1},
pi_r(d)=r!/[(r+1)^(r-1) product_i d_i!].
```

Append `d_r=0` and let `T(d)` be the unique rooted plane tree with preorder
outdegrees `(d_0,...,d_r)`.  Its root children have ordered branch sizes

```text
a(d)=(a_1,...,a_k),   a_i>=1,   sum_i a_i=r,   k=d_0,
```

where a branch size includes its root edge.  Let `Rroot_r` be the
stationary-flow projection of `Pbar_r` under `d -> a(d)`.

Put

```text
Z_s=(s+1)^(s-1)/s!,  Z_0=1,
w_a=Z_(a-1)=a^(a-1)/a!  for a>=1,
alpha_r=2/[3r(r-1)].
```

**Theorem 1 (root-composition projection).**  The stationary law of `Rroot_r`
on ordered compositions of `r` is

```text
rho_r(a_1,...,a_k)
 = [1/Z_r] [1/k!] product_i w_(a_i).                 (1.1)
```

For every adjacent pair `a_p,a_(p+1)`, let `a^merge` replace them by their
sum.  Then the exact two rates are

```text
Rroot_r(a,a^merge)=alpha_r k,                        (1.2)
Rroot_r(a^merge,a)=alpha_r
   w_(a_p)w_(a_(p+1))/w_(a_p+a_(p+1)).              (1.3)
```

Moreover, with the singleton convention at `r=1`,

```text
gap(Rroot_r) >= 2/[9 e r(r-1)]                      (1.4)
```

for every `r>=2`.

The projection in this statement is a stationary-flow projection.  It is
not claimed to be a strong lumping of `Pbar_r`.  Indeed reverse split rates
from two degree trees having the same coarse root composition can differ;
the smallest exact witness is given in Section 5.

## 2. Full-move child-boundary lemma

For a vertex `h` of `T(d)`, let its `k=d_h` child-subtree intervals be
`I_1,...,I_k` from left to right.  For every `p<k`, consider all upward
`Pbar_r` transitions generated by a word-adjacent pair of lower-`h`
factors corresponding to the attachment edges of `I_p,I_(p+1)`.

**Lemma 2.1 (exact boundary rate).**  For every `d,h,p` as above, the sum
of the rates of all those transitions is

```text
2d_h/[3r(r-1)].                                      (2.1)
```

Every such transition changes `d` to `d-e_h+e_j`, where `j` lies in
`I_p`; geometrically it merges child branches `p,p+1` by attaching the
second somewhere along the right spine of the first.  Every upward
off-diagonal transition belongs to exactly one such child boundary.

**Proof.**  Let `O_d` be the minimal long-cycle words with degree vector
`d`.  Stanley--Biane identifies `O_d` with all permutations of the parking
multiset having `d_h` copies of `h`, so

```text
|O_d|=r!/product_h d_h!.
```

The canonical interval-component lemma from M35 says that the factor chord
tree has exactly one edge from `h` into each `I_q`.  These are precisely the
factors whose lower endpoint is `h`.  Goulden--Yong clockwise-decreasing
factor labels put those `d_h` edges in the reverse of the child-interval
order when they are read in word order.  Thus a fixed consecutive pair of
occurrences among the lower-`h` factors is exactly a fixed adjacent child
boundary.

In a uniformly random permutation of a multiset of length `r` with `d_h`
copies of `h`, the gaps before, between, and after those copies are a
uniform weak composition of `r-d_h` into `d_h+1` parts.  A specified
internal gap is zero with probability

```text
binom(r-1,d_h-1)/binom(r,d_h)=d_h/r.
```

Consequently a fixed child boundary contributes exactly

```text
d_h |O_d|/r                                          (2.2)
```

marked source words and scan positions.

Write the corresponding ordered factor pair as

```text
(h,b)(h,j),   h<j<b,
```

where the second edge attaches the earlier child interval.  Its ordered
product has exactly the three decompositions

```text
(h,b)(h,j),  (h,j)(j,b),  (j,b)(h,b).
```

Both noncurrent outcomes, and only those, replace one lower label `h` by
`j`.  The interval order gives `j in I_p`.  The target preorder degree word
is feasible.  Immediately before the first vertex of `I_(p+1)` is read, the
open-vertex stack inside `I_p` consists exactly of the right spine of
`I_p`; decreasing `d_h` and increasing `d_j` keeps the preorder stack valid
exactly when `j` is one of those open vertices.  Thus the unique target plane
tree is obtained by merging the two branches and attaching `I_(p+1)` along
that right spine.  Dividing
(2.2) by `|O_d|`, then by the `r-1` scan sites and the three fibre outcomes,
and multiplying by the two favorable outcomes proves (2.1).

Conversely, every upward macro transition starts from an adjacent pair of
equal lower labels.  The two factors are two consecutive lower-`h`
attachment edges, hence determine one child boundary.  QED

Taking `h=0` in Lemma 2.1 gives (1.2), pointwise for every degree tree in a
fixed refined root composition.  If `h>0`, both endpoints lie in the same
root branch and the root composition is unchanged.

## 3. Stationary law and reverse normalization

The simply-generated weight of a plane tree is

```text
omega(t)=product_v 1/d_v(t)!,   sum_(t:|t|=s) omega(t)=Z_s.
```

Fix a root composition `a=(a_1,...,a_k)`.  The root contributes `1/k!`,
and after deleting the root edge of branch `i`, its remaining subtree has
`a_i-1` edges and total weight `Z_(a_i-1)=w_(a_i)`.  This proves (1.1).

If `a^merge` merges parts `A,B`, then

```text
rho_r(a)/rho_r(a^merge)=(1/k) w_A w_B/w_(A+B).       (3.1)
```

The stationary-flow projection is reversible.  Multiplying (1.2) by
(3.1) gives (1.3), with every factor `3r(r-1)` retained.

It is useful to record that the split factor is never superpolynomially
small.  Put `c=A+B`.  Then

```text
w_Aw_B/w_c
 = binom(c,A)(A/c)^A(B/c)^B c/(AB).                  (3.2)
```

The binomial probability at its integer mean is at least `1/(3sqrt(c))`,
and `AB<=c^2/4`, so

```text
w_Aw_B/w_c >= 4/(3c^(3/2)) >=4/(3r^(3/2)).          (3.3)
```

Thus every positive quotient split rate is at least
`8/[9r^(5/2)(r-1)]`.  Bound (3.3) is not needed for the sharper canonical
path proof below, but it calibrates the reverse normalization.

## 4. Canonical paths and exact basin accounting

From a composition `x=(x_1,...,x_q)`, repeatedly merge its first two parts
until reaching the one-part hub `H=(r)`.  For every ordered pair `x,y`, use
the path from `x` to `H` followed by the reverse of the path from `y` to
`H`.  Its length is at most `2(r-1)`.

Fix a merge edge

```text
s=(A,c_2,...,c_(ell+1)) -> (A+c_2,c_3,...,c_(ell+1)).
```

The sources whose first-merge path uses this edge are exactly

```text
(b_1,...,b_p,c_2,...,c_(ell+1)),
```

where `(b_1,...,b_p)` is an arbitrary ordered composition of `A`.  Define

```text
C_(A,p)=sum_(b_1+...+b_p=A) product_i w_(b_i).
```

The rooted-tree exponential generating function

```text
T(z)=sum_(a>=1) w_a z^a,   T(z)=z exp(T(z))
```

and Lagrange inversion give the exact identity

```text
C_(A,p)=[z^A]T(z)^p
       =p A^(A-p-1)/(A-p)!.                          (4.1)
```

Let `B_s` be the source basin of the edge.  Cancelling its fixed suffix in
(1.1) gives

```text
rho_r(B_s)/rho_r(s)
 =(ell+1)!/w_A sum_(p=1)^A C_(A,p)/(p+ell)!.
```

For every `p>=1`,

```text
(ell+1)!/(p+ell)! <=1/p!.
```

The root decomposition of all weighted plane trees with `A` edges says

```text
sum_(p=1)^A C_(A,p)/p!=Z_A.
```

Therefore

```text
rho_r(B_s)/rho_r(s)
 <=Z_A/w_A=Z_A/Z_(A-1)
 =(1+1/A)^(A-1)<=e.                                  (4.2)
```

This is the source-recovery and multiplicity accounting.  It is why the
one-part hub is harmless: it has stationary mass
`Z_(r-1)/Z_r=(r/(r+1))^(r-1)>=e^(-1)`, and the flow into every intermediate
edge has the local basin bound (4.2).

The stationary path length removes the extra worst-case factor `r`.  Put
`h(x)=number of parts of x minus one`.  Summing (1.1) over compositions of
fixed length, or equivalently using the root-degree law, gives

```text
h distributed as Bin(r-1,1/(r+1)),
E_rho h=(r-1)/(r+1)<1.                               (4.3)
```

Write the refined endpoint of a merge edge as
`s=(A,c_2,...,c_k)`, `k>=2`, and define its basin first moment

```text
M_s=sum_(x in B_s)rho_r(x)h(x).
```

The same fixed-suffix cancellation used above gives the exact formula

```text
M_s=rho_r(s)[k!/w_A]
    sum_p [(p+k-2)C_(A,p)/(p+k-1)!].                 (4.4)
```

For `p>=1,k>=2`,

```text
k!(p+k-2)/(p+k-1)! <=k/p!,                          (4.5)
```

because this is equivalent to
`p+k-2<=binom(p+k-1,p)`.  Equations (4.1)--(4.2) therefore give

```text
M_s<=e k rho_r(s).                                   (4.6)
```

Compare the one-step global `rho_r` heat bath, whose gap is one, to
`Rroot_r`.  For a commodity `(x,y)`, the path length is `h(x)+h(y)`.
The edge occurs in the first half exactly when `x in B_s` and in the second
half exactly when `y in B_s`; if both occur, both traversals are retained.
Thus its total length-weighted load is

```text
2[M_s+rho_r(B_s)E_rho h]
 <=2e(k+1)rho_r(s)
 <=3e k rho_r(s).                                    (4.7)
```

Its exact stationary capacity is

```text
Q_Rroot(s,s^merge)=rho_r(s) alpha_r k.               (4.8)
```

The congestion is at most `3e/alpha_r=9e r(r-1)/2`, proving (1.4).
This stationary-length sharpening was supplied in coordinator M41,
Section 11, from the exact formulas above and is rechecked here.  Every
route and every weight is computable in polynomial time; no exponentially
small canonical plane-tree shape is used.

## 5. Projection, not strong lumping

Forward merge rate (1.2) is pointwise independent of the internal branch
shapes.  Reverse split rates from an individual coarse degree tree need not
be independent of its shape, so `d -> a(d)` is not a strong lumping in both
directions.  At `r=3`, the two macros

```text
(0,1,1), (0,1,2)
```

both have coarse root composition `(3)`, but their total rates to refined
composition `(1,2)` are respectively `0` and `1/9`.  Stationary averaging
nevertheless gives the exact quotient split rate (1.3).  This distinction
prevents an unjustified Jensen mean-lift from the quotient back to full
`Pbar_r`.

For a fixed refined-to-coarse composition edge `a -> b`, the normalized
crossing flow has source marginal exactly `pi(.|a)`.  Its target marginal
`nu_(a,b)` satisfies

```text
d nu_(a,b)/d pi(.|b)
 =Pbar_r(d,F_a)/Rroot_r(b,a)
 <=1/Rroot_r(b,a)
 <=(9/8)r^(5/2)(r-1).                                (5.1)
```

Here the last inequality uses (1.3) and (3.3).  Thus the mean-lift bias is
polynomially absolutely continuous, but inserting (5.1) recursively at
every tree depth would multiply polynomial losses and does not by itself
prove a polynomial full gap.

## 6. Scope and exact falsification

The universal proof uses Stanley--Biane, the M35 interval-component lemma,
and the convention-checked Goulden--Yong incident-edge order.  The bounded
script `root_composition_audit.py` independently reconstructs every minimal
factorization, exact pair fibre, macro rate, plane tree, child boundary, and
root composition through `n=r+1<=8`.  It checks:

- no actual upward destination outside the predicted right spine;
- (2.1) for every vertex and every child boundary;
- (1.2)--(1.3) for every composition edge;
- the `r=2` rates `2/3,1/3`;
- (4.2); and
- the first reverse nonlumpability witness.

Finite passes are falsifiers only; Theorem 1 is proved above for every
`r`.  The smallest surviving full-projection implication is a recursive
restriction/mean-lift theorem that uses the product law of the child
subtrees without multiplying the Radon loss (5.1) through depth.
