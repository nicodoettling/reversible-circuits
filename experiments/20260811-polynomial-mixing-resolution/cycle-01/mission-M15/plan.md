## Objective and declared target

The global target is the uniform law on

`Omega(N,m) = {(t_1,...,t_m) in T_N^m : t_1...t_m = id}`

under the convention `(pq)(x)=p(q(x))`.  The reviewed kernel is

`K = (1/2)I + (1/(2|R_m|)) sum_r (1/(m-r+1)) sum_i H_{i,r}`,

where `R_m={3,4,5} intersect {1,...,m}` and each `H_{i,r}` is exact
same-product heat bath.  The mission is to locate quantitative primary-source
theorems that literally apply, reject tempting mismatched transfers, and build
one proof paradigm outside support-projection canonical paths.

## Expected failure modes

- A theorem concerns an unconditioned Cayley walk, not its endpoint bridge.
- A map bijection changes the stationary law or does not intertwine local
  transitions.
- A "restriction" kernel renormalizes accepted moves instead of rejecting
  exits into its diagonal, invalidating a direct decomposition citation.
- The aligned length-four subchain is reducible even when the full
  `{3,4,5}` chain is irreducible.
- A finite calculation is mistakenly promoted to an asymptotic gap theorem.
