# Cycle 05 plan: conditional-projector algebra and nonnormal red team

## Status and exact frontier

`STATUS: ACTIVE`.  With `M=binom(N,2)` and
`Q=P_transposition^2`, the independently accepted M10 recursion reduces the
aligned bridge theorem to the following one proposition:

> There are universal `c,C,C_0>0` such that every feasible fixed-endpoint
> microfactorized `Q` bridge of pair length
> `2<=l<=C_0 N log(N+1)` has Hamiltonian gap
> `Lambda_N(l;u,v)>=c(Nl)^(-C)`.

Pair lengths two, three, and four are closed, but the accepted general bound
`2M^(-(4l+4))` has an exponent growing with `l`.  This is the smallest open
implication.  It would imply a universal aligned-bridge gap and then Terminal
A through `gap(K)>=gap(B)/12`; it is not itself terminal until the complete
chain theorem, runtime, and TV accounting are integrated.

## Pivot reason

Two consecutive bridge cycles now reproduce the same barrier: qualitative
normal forms and full-state trees prove fixed-length connectivity/gaps but
pay exponentially in the growing base length.  This cycle therefore moves at
least half its effort to functional inequalities and exact transfer algebra,
