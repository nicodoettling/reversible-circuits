# A paired-source obstruction to polynomial-size local contour keys

Date: 2026-08-13 UTC.

## 1. Scope, target, and convention

For `d>=2`, let

```text
Z_d={(t_1,...,t_(2d-2)):t_1...t_(2d-2)=id,
                              support connected on [d]}.
```

Permutations compose as `(pq)(x)=p(q(x))`; tuple factors multiply
left-to-right. The signed right-Hurwitz actions are

```text
R_j(a,b)=(b,bab),       R_j^(-1)(a,b)=(aba,a).
```

By the accepted M112/M119 and M127/M130 DPS results, swapping adjacent edge
labels `i,i+1` descends from every rooted coordinate `(T,pi)` to one physical
involution `tau_i:Z_d->Z_d`. A fixed schedule is one word in the signed
generators whose positions and signs are independent of the source.

This note refutes one natural polynomial-cardinality metadata selector. It
does **not** refute the existence of some other polynomial schedule cover.

## 2. The tested polynomial contour summary

Fix the canonical lexicographically first rooted DPS preimage of `w` and the
two hairy-tree edges carrying labels `i,i+1`. Define `D_i(w)` to record:

1. the exact ordered physical factors `(w_i,w_(i+1))`;
2. the black/white endpoints of both labeled hairy edges;
3. the oriented first/second black endpoints of the two output faces;
4. the two directed boundary-traversal positions of each hairy edge in the
   accepted `+` contour; and
5. for each label, the numbers of distance-one and distance-three closures,
   its first and last global closure steps, and its distance-three step (or
   the empty marker).

This has polynomial raw cardinality. Put

```text
E=binom(d,2),
B=(2d-1)(2d-2),
S=(d-2)(2d-2).
```

Here `B` is the contour-token count and `S` the total closure count. A safe
fieldwise upper bound is

```text
|range(D_i)|
 <= E^2 [d(d-1)]^4 B^4 (S+1)^10
 = O(d^40).                                                (2.1)
```

The exponent is intentionally loose; only polynomiality matters. Thus if one
fixed polynomial-length schedule worked on every `D_i` class, this descriptor
would prove the assigned schedule-cover lemma.

## 3. Exact `d=4` collision

### Proposition 3.1 (one-schedule-per-`D_i` class; **REFUTED**)

At `d=4,i=1`, put

```text
A=(12), B=(13), C=(34), D=(23), E=(24),

x =(A,B,B,A,C,C),              y =(A,B,B,C,A,C),
x'=(A,D,B,E,D,C),              y'=(A,D,B,C,E,C).           (3.1)
```

Then

```text
tau_1(x)=x',       tau_1(y)=y',       D_1(x)=D_1(y),       (3.2)
```

but no fixed signed braid word sends both `x` to `x'` and `y` to `y'`.

**DPS certificate for (3.2).** Both canonical rooted sources use

```text
T={(1,2),(1,3),(2,4)},
pi_x=(3,2,1,4,6,5),       pi_y=(3,2,1,5,6,4).             (3.3)
```

Swapping values `1,2` gives `(3,1,2,4,6,5)` and
`(3,1,2,5,6,4)`. Independent hairy-tree closure gives exactly (3.1). The
common descriptor, with vertices one-based and contour/closure steps
zero-based, is

```text
physical pair:              ((12),(13))
hairy black/white endpoints:((2,2),(1,2))
oriented face endpoints:    ((2,1),(1,3))
boundary positions:         ((7,19),(2,20))
closure scalars:            ((1,1,5,11,{5}),
                             (1,1,4,10,{10})).             (3.4)
```

All four words are connected identity words of length six; this is also
checked directly by `schedule_cover_audit.py`.

## 4. Hand-checkable paired-subgroup proof

### Lemma 4.1 (diagonal Hurwitz subgroup invariant; **PROVED**)

For two `G`-factor tuples `u,v`, a common signed Hurwitz word preserves the
literal subgroup of `G x G` generated by their coordinatewise pairs.

**Proof.** Package the two tuples into a factorization in `G x G`. A right
Hurwitz action replaces adjacent factors `(a,b)` by `(b,b^(-1)ab)`. Both new
factors lie in `<a,b>`, and
`a=b(b^(-1)ab)b^(-1)` lies in the subgroup generated by the outputs. The
inverse action gives the same equality. Iterate over the schedule. QED.

For `(x,y)`, the coordinate-pair generators reduce to

```text
(A,A),(B,B),(A,C),(C,A),(C,C).                           (4.1)
```

The diagonal generators contain `Diag(S_4)`. Multiplying `(A,C)` by a
diagonal generator produces a double transposition in one coordinate; its
normal closure in `S_4` is the Klein four group `V_4`. Conversely every pair
in (4.1) has equal image in `S_4/V_4`, because `AC in V_4`. Hence the source
subgroup is exactly

```text
H=(V_4 x V_4) Diag(S_4),       |H|=16*24/4=96.           (4.2)
```

For `(x',y')`, the coordinate pairs include diagonal copies of
`A,D,B,C`, which generate `S_4`, together with `(E,C)`. Multiplying the latter
by `(C,C)` produces `(EC,id)`, where `EC` is a 3-cycle. Its normal closure is
`A_4`; diagonal multiplication then also gives `id x A_4`. Every generator
has equal parity in its two coordinates, so the target subgroup is exactly

```text
K=(A_4 x A_4) Diag(S_4)
 ={(p,q):sgn(p)=sgn(q)},        |K|=144*24/12=288.        (4.3)
```

Since `H!=K`, Lemma 4.1 forbids a common schedule and proves Proposition 3.1.

## 5. The schedule-conflict graph

For fixed `d,i`, define `C_(d,i)` on vertex set `Z_d`: join `u,v` when

```text
< (u_1,v_1),...,(u_m,v_m) >
 !=
< (tau_i(u)_1,tau_i(v)_1),..., (tau_i(u)_m,tau_i(v)_m) >. (5.1)
```

This graph supplies a clean necessary condition:

### Proposition 5.1 (**PROVED**)

Every one-schedule agreement set is independent in `C_(d,i)`. Consequently
every fixed-schedule cover has at least `chi(C_(d,i))` classes.

The converse is false as a proof method: an independent set merely passes
all paired-subgroup tests; it need not be simultaneously realizable by one
braid. The complete `d=3` calculation quantifies the gap. For every
`i=1,2,3`, the graph on 24 sources has

```text
80 edges,       omega=3,       chi=3,                    (5.2)
```

whereas exhaustive closure of the signed-Hurwitz action image of order 216
gives a true minimum schedule cover of five classes. Thus even an exact
polynomial coloring would still require a simultaneous-realization theorem.

The `d=4` pair in Section 3 is an edge whose endpoints lie in one `D_1`
class. In fact the finite audit finds such a size-two collision for every
adjacent index `i=1,...,5`; this is exact finite information only.

## 6. Why complete histories do not yet repair the proof

Every face label has exactly `d-2` closure events: it has `2d-3` loose
half-edges initially and one unmatched white half-edge finally, so closure
consumes the other `2d-4` in pairs. Recording all event steps and vertices
for the two swapped labels therefore has raw cardinality

```text
d^{O(d)},                                                   (6.1)
```

and recording the entire length-`B=O(d^2)` contour word has raw cardinality

```text
d^{O(d^2)}.                                                 (6.2)
```

These keys can distinguish the finite collision, but they do not yield a
polynomial schedule cover without a new compression theorem.

The sharp surviving statement is therefore:

> **CONFLICT-COLORING PLUS SIMULTANEOUS REALIZATION.** For every `d,i`, find
> `d^O(1)` classes covering `Z_d`, each independent in `C_(d,i)`, and prove
> that each class is contained in the agreement set of one signed braid word
> of length `d^O(1)`.

This is equivalent in force to the adjacent-value schedule-cover target but
separates its necessary invariant/coloring part from braid realization. It
is **UNRESOLVED**.

## 7. Conditional recovery and root quotient

Suppose such a cover has `q(d)=d^O(1)` fixed schedules of length at most
`L(d)=d^O(1)`, with a deterministic first-class assignment. Conditional on
`(i,class,scheduled time,direction)`, a directed physical action recovers the
source uniquely by inverting the fixed schedule prefix. Scheduled holds do
not affect this bijectivity. If holds are deleted and only compressed time is
retained, there are at most `L(d)` scheduled-time candidates.

There are `2d-3` adjacent indices. The rooted DPS lift has exactly `d` white
roots over every physical source, and deterministic root tracking pays the
one factor `d` audited by M122/M126. Thus class, time, index, compressed-time,
and root losses would all remain polynomial. This would settle only the
adjacent-permutation coordinate. It would not route UST basis exchanges or
prove the full rooted product comparison, any physical gap, `Gamma_0`, the
original heat-bath gap, or a Terminal outcome.
