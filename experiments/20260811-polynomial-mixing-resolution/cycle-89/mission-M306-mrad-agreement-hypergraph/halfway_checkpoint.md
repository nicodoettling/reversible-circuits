# M306 Halfway Checkpoint

Date: 2026-08-14 UTC

- M301/M304 and M296/M299 source locks pass in full.
- The predeclared `(RCOM)` conditional-expectation identity is exact and is a
  sufficient condition for MRAD.
- A strict obstruction has emerged: `(RCOM)` is not polynomially equivalent
  to MRAD.  In the full symmetric or alternating action on `N` points, the
  identity matching has an exact one-action cover, but every anchored RCOM
  score on the full matching is exactly `2`.  Taking
  `N=d^(d-3)(2d-2)!/d!` separates the two by a superpolynomial factor.
- The corrected exact formulation is weighted residual agreement / the LP
  fractional action-cover number.  It is polynomially equivalent to MRAD up
  to `H_|M|=O(d log d)` and does not average away rare high-agreement actions.
- M301/M304's actual `d=3` witness is retained: its three pair intersections
  are distinct singleton actions and the triple is empty, so no Helly step is
  introduced.

No actual-M135 MRAD refutation or proof is claimed.  Literal braid length
remains separate.
