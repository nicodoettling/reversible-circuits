# M337 plan — weighted actual-family triple-shadow decay

Date: 2026-08-14 UTC

## Objective

Continue the independently accepted M328/M331 triple-shadow lane. Using only
the actual residual families and their exact whole-child triple shadows,
derive the strongest weight-dependent pivot-averaging identity and decide a
standalone RATSD/low-density-terminality lemma. Prove a conditional
RATSD-plus-terminality implication or freeze the first exact obstruction.

## Declared object

This is a deterministic weighted finite-family problem, not a sampler target.
Weights are arbitrary nonnegative functions on the authenticated actual
family. Every quantifier over the positive heavy anchor, admissible `B,C`, and
weighted instance is universal. Zero total weight is handled separately and
never used as a heavy-anchor witness.

## Candidate schema to freeze before computation

After authenticating and importing the exact M328/M331 notation, freeze one
literal candidate with all of the following fields:

1. the currently active pivot and its actual admissible children;
2. each child's **whole** triple shadow, without replacing it by an induced
   graph or pairwise neighborhood;
3. arbitrary nonnegative input weights and the exact weighted-average loss
   identity across children;
4. a universal positive heavy anchor and explicit `B,C` exponents;
5. an iterative alternative: either a child retains the required weight and
   advances the actual family, or a quantitatively low-density terminal
   configuration is reached;
6. the final-step weight loss and stopping quantifiers, including the case in
   which the last active pivot has no admissible child.

The fully quantified statement will be written in `candidate.md` before any
guard. No computation is authorized until that file is frozen.

## Analytic route

1. SHA-lock M296/M299, M301/M304, M306/M309, M312/M315, M317/M320,
   M324/M327, and M328/M331.
2. Reconstruct the accepted actual-family child-neighborhood/triple-shadow
   identity and the M324 active-pivot correction.
3. Average the exact child losses with arbitrary weights; keep the final child
   shadow intact.
4. Test whether the resulting recurrence yields the frozen RATSD/terminality
   conclusion with polynomial `d^B,d^C` losses.
5. If a clause fails, stop at the first standalone weighted obstruction and
   record the strongest surviving identity.

## Firewalls

- Residual incompatibility means exactly empty
  `D_I(Q) intersection D_I(R)` on the actual family; it is not an M135
  conflict edge.
- Do not replace a whole child triple shadow by an induced graph.
- Do not use unweighted MIDEC, assume polynomial support, infer pair
  compatibility from conflict independence/transitivity/moments, or promote
  rank-3 boundary examples.
- No broad `d=6` census. At most one single guard may be predeclared only
  after the analytic candidate is frozen.
- A heavy proper compatible subcell may prove WRAD directly but is not relabeled
  HPRN or PHTC.
- No HPRN, WRAD, routing, gap, mixing, or Terminal inference without an actual
  proof and subsequent fresh audit.

## Deliverable

explicit next lemma, reproducible provenance, and a request for a genuinely
