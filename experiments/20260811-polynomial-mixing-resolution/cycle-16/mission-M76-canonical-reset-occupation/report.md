## 2. Proved phase geometry and exact reachability

Put

```text
u=K-R,                    P(x)=s+u.
```

A deep child arising from a `k`-part root composition with last part `t`
and attachment rank `j>=1` is

```text
y=(t-1,K+k,R+j,B).
```

If `n=s-t` is the prefix mass, its `k-1` positive parts give `n>=k-1`,
and therefore

```text
P(x)-P(y)=s-t+1-k+j=n-k+j+1>=j>=1.                 (2.1)
```

A used canonical edge has refined endpoint
`(A,c_2,...,c_k)` and merges its first two parts. Its residual child is

```text
y=(A+c_2-1,k-1,k-2,c_2),
P(y)=A+c_2<=s<P(x).                                 (2.2)
```

Thus `D+J` is a finite DAG under the strictly descending integer `P`; all
resolvents in (1.1) are finite algebraic sums, not analytic limits.

Every reset child has `K-R=1`. Starting at the seed `(K,R)=(1,0)`, one
deep step with `k=K'-1,j=R'` reaches every `K'>R'>=1`. Conversely `j>=1`
precludes every formal mark `R=0,K>1`. Hence the exact reachable closure is

```text
{(1,0)} union {(K,R):K>R>=1}.                       (2.3)
```

This matters in potential scans: the worst formal inverse-weight rows with
`R=0,K>1` are not generated by canonical resets.

## 3. Fixed-power one-step certificates are universally false

Fix any finite exponent `d>=0` and take the right-heavy family

```text
x_s=(s,K_s,K_s-1,s-1),          K_s=s^2.
```

The unary root composition supplies a deep child with coefficient exactly
`K_s/(K_s+1)`, child `P=s`, child `u=1`, and child suffix excess plus one
`ell=1`. The canonical degree-two edge `(s-1,1)->(s)` has source basin
`Z={last part=1}` and reset child `(s-1,1,0,1)`. On `Z`, the parent
contextual mean is zero. Since the edge capacity equals `q_x`, its reset
coefficient is exactly

```text
p_s^2,               p_s=rho_s(last part=1).
```

The single composition `(s-1,1)` gives

```text
p_s>=w_(s-1)/(2w_(s+1))->e^(-2)/2.                 (3.1)
```

Both displayed children have `P=s`. Therefore the one-step ratio for
`V=P^d u` has lower limit

```text
1+e^(-4)/4>1.                                       (3.2)
```

For `V=P^d u[1+log ell]`, the same two terms give the lower bound

```text
(s/(s+1))^d [K_s/(K_s+1)+p_s^2(1+log(s-1))]
              /(1+log 2),                          (3.3)
```

which diverges. Thus **every fixed exponent** in both proposed one-step
families is refuted. This is not an occupation counterexample: the reset
lands at `B=1`, and the subsequent deep excursion is exactly the
cancellation discarded by a one-step maximum.

## 4. Exact reset renewal and the inverse-split-mass reduction


Every maximum occurs at `(K,R)=(1,0)`. These values are exact finite-DAG
floating evaluations and prove no asymptotic inequality.

The structurally natural potential is the reciprocal stationary split
mass

```text
h(x)=1/q_x=w_(s+1)/[w_(s+1-B)w_B].                 (4.1)
```

For a deep child, `q_y=w_(t-B)w_B/w_t`; for a canonical reset child in
(2.2), `q_y=w_Aw_(c_2)/w_(A+c_2)`. Thus the factors in (4.1) cancel the
same split weights already present in `D` and `J`.

The decisive remaining statement is

```text
sum_y K_reset(x,y) h(y) <= h(x)                     (4.2)
```

for every right-heavy `x`. The accepted M50 split estimate gives

```text
1<=h(x)<=(3/4)(s+1)^(3/2).                          (4.3)
```

Equation (4.2) is sufficient for polynomial occupation. Indeed `D` is
substochastic and strictly lowers `P`, while M71 proved

```text
a(x)<=e(1+log s)min(s,K-R)<=eP(1+log P).
```

Consequently every pure-deep base excursion obeys
`b(x)<=eP^2(1+log P)`. Since `J` lands right-heavy, (4.2) iterates:
if `mu_n=delta_x K_reset^n`, then `mu_n h<=h(x)` and hence
`mu_n 1<=h(x)`. Strict `P` descent makes `mu_n=0` for `n>=P(x)`. Therefore
the full separated marked base occupation is at most

```text
sum_(n<P) mu_n b
 <=e P^3(1+log P)h(x)
 <=(3e/4)P^(9/2)(1+log P).                          (4.4)
```

M71's one-shot `O(r^9)` actual-edge label Cauchy bound would then make the
unmarking loss polynomial as well.

Finite evidence isolates why (4.2), rather than a raw inequality, is the
right target. Through `s=14`, `K=1,...,16,64,256,4096`, the largest
eliminated inverse-`q` ratio is `.761049`, at

the raw `(D+J)h/h` ratio is already `1.08505` there, and the raw ratio
including `a` is `1.14727`. Pure-deep elimination is essential.

## 5. Proved one-dimensional seed-kernel identity

For a seed `(K,R)=(1,0)`, define the Cayley coefficients

```text
A_a=[z^a](e^T-1)/T
   =sum_(p-part compositions of a) product w/(p+1)!,

C_a=[z^a]int_0^1 u e^(uT)du
   =[z^a]((T-1)e^T+1)/T^2
   =sum_(p-part compositions of a)(p+1)product w/(p+2)!.
```

For the degree-two canonical reset with child suffix `t`, basin summation
gives

```text
p_t=P(last=t)=w_t A_(s-t)/w_(s+1),
q_B=w_(s+1-B)w_B/w_(s+1),

M_B(t)=0,                                           t<B,
       =w_B A_(s-B)/w_(s+1),                       t=B,
       =w_(t-B)w_B C_(s-t)/w_(s+1),               t>B.
```

The exact residual entry is

```text
J_s(B,t)=[M_B(t)-q_Bp_t]^2
          /{[w_(s-t)w_t/w_(s+1)]q_B}.              (5.1)
```

Proof: the canonical basin consists of all ordered refinements of its
merged first part. Summing `rho(H-q_B)` over those refinements gives
`M_B(t)-q_Bp_t`; the normalized edge capacity is the displayed split
flow. Expanding by the number of prefix parts produces `A` and `C`.

The child rank is `s-1`; (5.1) is not a same-rank loop. It agrees with
every direct composition entry through `s=10` within `2e-12`. The scaled
implementation uses `v_a=e^{-a}w_a`: `p_t,M_B,Q_G` each acquire exactly
one factor `e^{-1}`, while `q_B` is invariant. A literal scaled/unscaled
assertion at `s=12` has maximum relative coefficient error `5.68e-16` and
kernel error `8.63e-14`.

## 8. First unproved lemma and strongest self-attack

> **DEEP-ELIMINATED INVERSE-SPLIT-MASS SUPERSOLUTION.** For every
> `s>=2`, `K>=1`, `1<=B<=s`, and right-heavy seed
> `x=(s,K,K-1,B)`, let `D,J_can` have the exact normalizations in Field 1.
> Then
> ```text
> sum_y [(I-D)^(-1)J_can](x,y)/q_y <=1/q_x.          (8.1)
> ```

A relaxed factor `1+C/P(x)` also suffices, because `P` decreases at every
reset and its product is polynomial.

No martingale proof is being smuggled into (8.1). Canonical basin currents
are not orthogonal Doob increments: the raw inverse-`q` inequality fails,
one-step `a+delta` exceeds one from the bounded rank-10 scan onward, and the
eliminated reset mass is not bounded by `Var(H)/q` in the tested seeds.
The finite `.761` margin can shrink or reverse after `s=14`; it is evidence
for selecting (8.1), not evidence that (8.1) is true.

Conversely, the one-step counterfamily in Field 3 does not refute (8.1).
Its reset child changes the suffix phase from `B=s-1` to `B=1`, precisely
where the eliminated inverse-`q` weighting gains room. An actual obstruction
must evaluate the full pure-deep excursion or exhibit an infinite family
violating (8.1), not reuse the raw two-child ratio.
