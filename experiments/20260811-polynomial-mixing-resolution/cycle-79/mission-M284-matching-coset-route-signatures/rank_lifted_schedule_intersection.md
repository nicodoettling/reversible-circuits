# Rank-lifted schedule intersections and the chart-coverage obstruction

Date: 2026-08-14 UTC.  Status: **proved strict word-level lifting lemma and
analytic obstruction to the naive local-type cover; the M135 polynomial
matching-coset refinement remains unresolved**.

## 1. Scope, target, and conventions

For `d>=5`, put `m=2d-2` and

```text
Z_d={(t_1,...,t_m):t_1...t_m=id, support connected on [d]}.
```

Permutations compose as `(p q)(x)=p(q(x))`.  Factors, signed Hurwitz
generators, and schedules are applied chronologically from left to right:

```text
R_j[a,b]=[b,bab],       R_j^(-1)[a,b]=[aba,a].       (1.1)
```

Write `beta;gamma` for the schedule which first applies `beta` and then
`gamma`.  Let `B_m` be the braid group generated by these signed symbols on
length-`m` words, and let

```text
phi_d:B_m -> A_d                                      (1.2)
```

be its finite action image on `Z_d`.  The target in this note is an exact
deterministic endpoint/fixed-schedule statement.  There is no probability
law or Markov kernel here, and no congestion, gap, or mixing conclusion.

The assigned M135/M137, M175/M176, M181/M183, M200/M202, M240/M242,
M269/M271, M273/M275, and M278/M281 inventories all verify.  Their exact top
digests are in `checkpoint.md`.

## 2. M135 action fibers and their word-level preimages

Fix an adjacent value index `i`.  For an M135 `H`-orbit `Q`, choose
`w_Q in Q` and recall the action fiber

```text
K_Q={a in A_d:a(w_Q)=tau_i(w_Q)}.                    (2.1)
```

Because braid actions commute with global relabeling and `tau_i` commutes
with the accepted root stabilizer `H`, this is independent of the chosen
representative.  Define its word-level preimage

```text
Ktilde_Q={beta in B_m:beta(w_Q)=tau_i(w_Q)}
        =phi_d^(-1)(K_Q).                            (2.2)
```

For any family `S` of `H`-orbits,

```text
intersection_(Q in S) K_Q is nonempty
iff
intersection_(Q in S) Ktilde_Q is nonempty.          (2.3)
```

Indeed a common braid element gives a common action.  Conversely a common
action has a braid representative, and every representative has the same
endpoint action on all of `Z_d`.  A polynomial word-length requirement is a
bound on the signed-generator length of a representative in the right-hand
intersection.

## 3. The exact type distinction at one rank extension

Let `T` be a Cayley tree on `[d]`, rooted at the leaf `d`, with root edge
`{1,d}`.  Use M278's canonical coordinate

```text
pi_d=(2d-3,2d-2,1,2,...,2d-4)
```

and put

```text
u_T  =F_d(T,pi_d),
u'_T =F_d(T,s_i pi_d)=tau_i^(d)(u_T),
k_d  =(0,d),

U_T  =u_T k_d k_d
     =F_(d+1)(T+{{1,d+1}},pi_(d+1)),
N_T  =u'_T k_d k_d,
A_T  =F_(d+1)(T+{{1,d+1}},s_i pi_(d+1))
     =tau_i^(d+1)(U_T).                              (3.1)
```

M278/M281 supplies a signed word `c_(T,i) in B_(m+2)` satisfying exactly

```text
c_(T,i)(N_T)=A_T.                                   (3.2)
```

Equation (3.2) is an **old-edited-padded to new-edited** correction.  Its
input is `N_T`, not the unedited upper source `U_T`.  Therefore M278 does not
assert

```text
c_(T,i)(U_T)=tau_i^(d+1)(U_T),                       (3.3)
```

and `c_(T,i)` alone is not licensed as an element of the upper M135 fiber.
The objects also have different types:

```text
K_Q       subset A_d,       actions on length m;
c_(T,i)   element B_(m+2),  a word on length m+2;
K_(Q+)    subset A_(d+1),   actions on length m+2.   (3.4)
```

This is the required firewall between the accepted local theorem and the
same-rank matching-coset obligation.

## 4. Rank-lifted schedule-intersection lemma

Let

```text
j_d:B_m -> B_(m+2),       j_d(R_r^(+/-1))=R_r^(+/-1)   (4.1)
```

be the standard injective braid inclusion which leaves the last two strands
fixed.  Let `Q_T` be the lower `H`-orbit of `u_T` and `Q_T^+` the upper
`H`-orbit of `U_T`.

Define the literal liftable word fiber

```text
L_(T,i)
 ={j_d(beta);c_(T,i): beta in Ktilde_(Q_T)}
 subset B_(m+2).                                    (4.2)
```

### Theorem 4.1 (rank-lifted endpoint fibers; **PROVED**)

For every accepted chart `(T,i)`,

```text
L_(T,i) subset Ktilde_(Q_T^+).                       (4.3)
```

For a finite family of accepted charts on which the literal correction word
is one common word `c`, one moreover has the exact braid-set identity

```text
intersection_T {j_d(beta);c:beta in Ktilde_(Q_T)}

 ={j_d(beta);c:
      beta in intersection_T Ktilde_(Q_T)}.          (4.4)
```

Consequently, if one explicit lower word `beta` serves every member of the
family, then the one explicit upper word `j_d(beta);c` serves every extended
member.  If `|beta|<=L` and `|c|<=ell`, its displayed length is at most
`L+ell`.

#### Proof

Take `beta in Ktilde_(Q_T)`.  It maps `u_T` to `u'_T`.  The embedded word
`j_d(beta)` applies the same moves to the first `m` positions and never
touches the final square.  Using `(3.1)`,

```text
j_d(beta)(U_T)=j_d(beta)(u_T k_d k_d)
              =u'_T k_d k_d=N_T.                   (4.5)
```

Apply `(3.2)` next.  The chronological concatenation maps `U_T` to
`A_T=tau_i^(d+1)(U_T)`, proving `(4.3)` at the chosen representative.
Braid/global-conjugation commutation and upper `H`-equivariance of `tau_i`
extend the endpoint equality to its full `H`-orbit.

For `(4.4)`, the forward inclusion from right to left is immediate.  In the
other direction, suppose one braid element has representations

```text
j_d(beta_T);c
```

for every `T`.  Right cancellation by the common `c` makes all
`j_d(beta_T)` equal.  Injectivity of `j_d` makes all `beta_T` equal to one
`beta`, which belongs to every lower word fiber.  This proves `(4.4)` and
the common-word consequence.  The length bound is literal concatenation.
QED.

### Action-image qualification

Identity `(4.4)` is an exact statement about identically factored braid
elements.  The corresponding upper action sets are

```text
phi_(d+1)(L_(T,i)) subset K_(Q_T^+).                 (4.6)
```

Different factored braid elements can induce the same upper action, so
intersections of the sets in `(4.6)` can be larger than the image of the
literal intersection `(4.4)`.  Thus Theorem 4.1 is a sufficient recursive
construction, not a classification of every possible upper common action.
No converse or lower bound on M135 refinement is claimed.

## 5. The minimally safe recursive route signature

A rank-`d` action `a in A_d` is not by itself safe recursive state.  The
standard braid inclusion acts on braid elements.  It descends to a map

```text
A_d -> A_(d+1)
```

only if

```text
j_d(ker phi_d) subset ker phi_(d+1),                 (5.1)
```

and no accepted inventory proves `(5.1)`.  Even when two lower words have
the same lower action, their embeddings need not have a licensed common
action on all of `Z_(d+1)`.  An explicit word is also necessary to retain a
quantitative length.

The smallest state licensed by the current theorems is therefore:

```text
route signature =
  (valid rooted extension chart,
   explicit lower braid beta or full word fiber Ktilde_Q,
   literal local correction c).                    (5.2)
```

For one source the exact semantic state is the translated embedded set
`L_(T,i)` in `(4.2)` (or its upper action image `(4.6)` when only actions
matter).  For a selected route the recurrence is simply

```text
beta^+ = j_d(beta);c.                                (5.3)
```

Neither the local correction `c` nor the lower action fiber `K_Q` alone
contains enough information for `(5.3)`.  The chart is also material: it is
the certificate for the three separate endpoint identities in `(3.1)--(3.2)`.

## 6. The complete local correction alphabet has size at most two

The accepted table can be standardized without retaining M240's optional
absent-child signs.  For an ordinary odd edit

```text
i=2v-3,       2<=v<=d-1,       L=d-v-1,
```

put

```text
Q^-_(d,v)=product_(t=0)^(L-1)
 R_(2(v+t)-2)^(-1) R_(2(v+t)-1)^(-1),               (6.1)
```

with the empty product when `L=0`, and retain M240's five-move carrier

```text
B_d=R_(2d-4)^(-1) R_(2d-3) R_(2d-2)^(-1)
    R_(2d-3)^(-1) R_(2d-4)^(-1).                    (6.2)
```

M240's scan proof uses only

```text
direct larger child => the sign at that slot is negative,                (6.3)
```

and explicitly allows either sign at a nonchild.  Choosing every sign
negative therefore gives one tree-independent open word

```text
c_open(d,v)=Q^-_(d,v);B_d;(Q^-_(d,v))^(-1),          (6.4)
```

of length `4(d-v-1)+5<=4d-7`.  A nondecreasing/closed path has `N_T=A_T`
and uses the identity.

Together with M278, the exact fixed-`(d,i)` alphabet is:

| edit family | indices | correction words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ordinary odd | `i=2v-3`, `2<=v<=d-1` | `id` or `c_open(d,v)` |
| internal even | `i=2,4,...,2d-6` | `id` |
| cross boundary | `i=2d-4` | `(R_(2d-5)^(-1))^2` |
| last | `i=2d-3` | `R_(2d-2)^(-1)` |

These four rows partition every `1<=i<=2d-3`, including `v=d-1`, the
minimal old rank `d=5`, the physical cross-boundary seam, and the last
new-child/root seam.  Hence

```text
|C_(d,i)|<=2                                           (6.5)
```

for every fixed local rank-extension problem.  A correction is common only
inside a class on which the **literal word** in this table is fixed.

### Corollary 6.1 (one-step conditional class recurrence; **PROVED**)

Suppose an extension-compatible lower family is covered by `q` explicit
fixed braid schedules of length at most `L`, and every upper source has one
of the valid charts above.  Refining by the exact local word gives at most

```text
2q                                                   (6.6)
```

upper fixed-schedule classes, each with a displayed schedule of length at
most `L+4d-7`.

**Proof.**  Partition first by the lower schedule and then by the at-most-two
literal correction words.  Apply Theorem 4.1 in each cell.  QED.

This is a one-step theorem for a declared extension-compatible family.  It
does not assert that such a family is an M135 perfect-matching color, and it
does not turn an arbitrary sequence of rank lifts into a polynomial number
of classes without a separate compatibility/compression argument.

## 7. Arbitrary-rank obstruction to the naive chart/local-type cover

The local alphabet is small, but its chart domain is not all of `Z_d`.
Here is an analytic family showing the exact failure.

Fix an upper rank `D>=6` and put

```text
e_r=(0,r),                 1<=r<=D-1,
U_D=e_1,e_1,e_2,e_2,...,e_(D-1),e_(D-1).             (7.1)
```

This is the canonical doubled-star word.  It is an unedited M278 extension
source: use at old rank `D-1` the star rooted at `D-1` and then add the new
root leaf `D` at vertex `1` (one-based notation).  Apply one right Hurwitz
move at positions two and three:

```text
V_D=R_2(U_D)
   =e_1,e_2,e_2 e_1 e_2,e_2,e_3,e_3,...,
     e_(D-1),e_(D-1).                                (7.2)
```

### Proposition 7.1 (chart/local-type-only coverage; **REFUTED**)

For every `D>=6`, `V_D` lies in the minimal connected identity sector
`Z_D`, but no global conjugate of `V_D` is an unedited M278 extension-chart
source.  Consequently, for every adjacent index and every M135 perfect-
matching color at rank `D`, refinement only by the M278 chart and local
correction type fails to cover that color.

#### Proof

Every factor in `(7.2)` is a transposition.  Its first four factors have
product

```text
e_1 e_2 (e_2 e_1 e_2) e_2
=e_1 e_1=id,                                         (7.3)
```

and every later factor occurs in an adjacent square.  Thus the whole product
is identity.  The support contains every star edge `e_r` (and the extra
triangle edge `e_2 e_1 e_2`), so it is connected on all `D` labels.  Its
length is exactly `2D-2`, hence it is in the declared minimal sector.

M278's canonical-slot lemma says every unedited chart source has the form

```text
f_1,f_1,f_2,f_2,...,f_(D-1),f_(D-1).                 (7.4)
```

In particular its first two factors are equal.  Simultaneous global
conjugation is injective on transpositions and preserves equality at each
fixed pair of positions.  But the first two factors of `V_D` are the
distinct edges `e_1,e_2`.  Therefore no global conjugate of `V_D` has the
chart form `(7.4)`.

For fixed `(D,i)`, an M135 matching color is a perfect matching of its
orbit-incidence graph.  Its left vertices are all global `G`-orbits in
`Z_D`, so left-perfectness puts exactly one `H`-orbit edge of the color over
the left vertex `[V_D]_G`.  No representative of that edge can have the
chart form, again by the pair-equality invariant.  Hence the local chart/type
does not cover the color.  QED.

This proposition refutes only the named **chart/local-type-only** use of the
local table.  It is not a lower bound on the true number of M135 schedule
classes: an additional source-dependent base route could leave the chart
domain and might still yield a polynomial cover.

There is a second, independent compatibility warning even on a charted
global orbit.  A perfect-matching color may select any one of its `D`
root-stabilizer orbit edges, while the chart identifies one distinguished
rooted edge.  Only `H`-equivariance, not arbitrary-root synchronization, is
accepted.  M200/M202 resolves the analogous root-pair coverage only for
M181's width-two family at finite rank `d=5`.

## 8. Exact class-count decision and relation to voltage

The results above determine exactly what the accepted local table contributes:

```text
valid extension-compatible q-class family
  -> at most 2q explicit upper classes, length +<=4d-7;        PROVED

one arbitrary M135 perfect-matching color
  -> not covered by the chart/local-type signature;                    REFUTED

polynomial common-action refinement of the full color
  -> unresolved.                                                       (8.1)
```

In particular, it would be incorrect to report either a polynomial upper
bound or a superpolynomial lower bound from this mission.  The surviving
full-color problem requires additional base-route/canonicalization data for
the orbit `[V_D]` and every other chartless orbit, plus compatibility with
the `H`-edge actually selected by the matching.

M181/M183's voltage theorem remains conditional on the matched quotient
target lying in the source synchronized component.  The local table does not
place arbitrary selected matching targets in that component.  M200/M202's
every-color conclusion remains exact finite `d=5` for one width-two family
and does not provide an all-rank chart cover.  No voltage-length conclusion
is imported here.

## 9. Frozen standalone lemma and first remaining obligation

The durable proved object is Theorem 4.1:

> **RANK-LIFTED SCHEDULE-INTERSECTION LEMMA.**  On a declared family of
> valid rooted extension charts with a common literal local correction,
> identically factored upper schedules are exactly the common lower braid
> schedules under standard embedding followed by that correction.  A
> `q`-class lower cover therefore lifts through the complete accepted local
> table to at most `2q` classes in one rank step, with additive length at
> most `4d-7`.

The first missing interface for the M135 goal is now explicit: construct a
polynomial-cardinality **base-route signature cover** which includes
chartless left global orbits and the actual `H`-edges selected by an
arbitrary perfect-matching color, and whose translated embedded upper action
sets have nonempty common intersections with polynomial representatives.
Theorem 4.1 can then concatenate its local terminal pieces.  This statement
remains unresolved; Proposition 7.1 shows that its base-route/chartless clause
cannot be deleted.

The local theorem starts at old rank `d>=5`, hence the arbitrary-rank color
obstruction is stated at upper rank `D>=6`.  `D=5` remains governed only by
the accepted finite statements cited above.  The auxiliary word length is
always the minimal even length `2D-2`; odd length, `m=0`, `m=2`, and `N=2`
are outside this rooted minimal-sector rank-lift statement and receive no new
claim.
