# M285 plan: owner-correlation inequality

## Objective

Starting from the freshly audited exact first-cell identity, prove or refute
the all-rank per-channel inequalities

```text
eta_1(F_ell)>=0,   eta_2(F_ell)>=0
```

for every odd `k>=3`, `n=3k`, and `2<=ell<=n-2`.  This is the OCI/CP
question for the first separated fixed cell of the auxiliary minimal
long-cycle factorization law, not a global identity-bridge theorem.

## Strict proof workflow

1. Record this plan and initial checkpoint before opening substantive source
   artifacts.
2. Verify the immutable M280/M283 and exact M91/M94, M261/M264 inventories.
3. Reconstruct the accepted identity
   `CP_j=Lambda_n eta_j(F_ell)/[(n-3)(n-4)]`.
4. Disintegrate `eta_j(F_ell)` by residual owner-cycle sizes and literal
   cyclic phase before any sign or absolute value, retaining source kind
   `T/D` and target `IS/IN/X`.
5. Seek an exact Cayley/Borel pairing, positive-semidefinite correlation
   representation, or total-positivity argument.
6. If OCI is false, freeze an analytic counterfamily or the first exact
   counterexample.  If it stays open, freeze the narrowest explicit
   coefficient inequality and state the weaker cross-channel negative-mass
   fallback.

## Computation budget

At most one compact new exact falsifier, chosen only after the symbolic
formula and failure criterion are fixed.  A positive finite row is never an
all-rank proof.

## Proof obligations

- explicit convention, odd-rank and lag endpoints;
- exact source/channel and Borel normalizations;
- no deletion of triangle/double, `IS/IN/X`, collision, or decorated terms;
- standalone first unproved inequality if the sign does not close;
- boundary/minimal-case falsification;
- no FCL1 inference unless a complete auditable all-rank OCI theorem is
  obtained.

## Scope firewall

This mission edits only its own directory.  It makes no later-cell,
direct-L1/H0, spectral-gap, polynomial-mixing, or Terminal claim.
