# Mission M274 plan: minority-sign closure

## Objective

Pursue the accepted M168/M171 minority-sign substitute as an independent
route from the proved M266/M268 PCS estimate to the fixed-cell first-level
bound (`FCL1`, equivalently the required `A_k(1,1)` control), without assuming
the unresolved phase-boundary variation / fixed-cell derivative route
(`PBV`/`FD`/`SET`).

## Claim discipline

- Immediate target law: the auxiliary uniform minimal-length factorization
  law of the long cycle, viewed through M73's nonlazy cyclic rank-two
  quotient kernel and restricted to the first separated fixed cell.  The
  downstream application is to FCL1 for the global bridge program, but this
  mission does not analyze the lazy `{3,4,5}` identity heat-bath kernel.
- Source lock: immutable M161/M166, M168/M171, M174/M178, M266/M268, plus
  every exact fixed-cell dependency actually cited.  All hashes will be
  checked before use.
- First recover the literal minority-sign lemma with every lag, Abel channel,
  `T/D` kind, coefficient, normalization, and boundary term.  No shorthand
  implication may be promoted before this reconstruction.
- Determine the weakest one-sided/sign or minority-mass statement that,
  together with proved PCS, yields FCL1.  Keep sufficiency distinct from
  necessity.
- Attempt an analytic proof from the exact critical-Borel / phase law.  No
  sign may be inferred from finite enumeration, plotting, or generic
  total-positivity intuition.
- A tiny computation is permitted only after a universal statement and
  parameter range are fixed; it is a falsifier, not proof.
- If the natural premise fails, seek an exact arbitrary-rank or asymptotic
  counterfamily and state the minimally repaired one-sided lemma.
- No bridge gap, mixing, or terminal conclusion follows in this mission
  unless every downstream accepted implication is separately cited and the
  new lemma survives fresh immutable audit.

## Work plan

1. Read and hash all immutable source artifacts; reconstruct definitions and
   conventions independently.
2. Restate the exact M168 minority-sign reduction and M171 audit conclusion,
   then algebraically identify the precise sign/minority-mass input needed
   beside PCS.
3. Translate that input into the literal fixed-cell critical-Borel phase
   representation from M161/M166 and M174/M178, retaining both Abel channels,
   endpoint kinds, and all boundary corrections.
4. Try a direct analytic sign or one-sided-mass proof using exact recurrences,
   involutions, and phase pairing.  Record the first unproved inequality.
5. If warranted, predeclare and run the smallest exact falsifier on boundary,
   parity, and near-central cases.  Upgrade any finite failure to an analytic
   family or leave the repaired statement explicitly conditional.
6. Freeze a theorem, strict reduction, or counterexample report; compute
   hashes and request a fresh immutable audit if a promotable claim results.

## Deliverables

- `checkpoint.md` with source-lock and halfway status;
- a detailed minority-sign reconstruction/proof note;
- optional exact falsifier and machine-readable output;
- `report.md`, `fresh_audit_request.md` when appropriate,
  `freeze_manifest.md`, and `SHA256SUMS`.

## Expected failure modes

- confusing a bound on the signed sum with a bound on its minority sign mass;
- dropping an exterior power of `n` or an endpoint/central lag term;
- proving a statement for one Abel channel that fails after channel
  combination;
- taking absolute values before the cancellation encoded by PCS;
- silently replacing the critical-Borel phase law by an independent model;
- using finite sign evidence as if it established a universal inequality.
