Independently audit the immutable M140 scalar Cayley lemma and corrected
auxiliary right-heavy suffix-excess-five certificate.  The claim under review
is auxiliary only: it is not an auxiliary gap, ROOT-MEAN LIFT, an
original-chain gap, polynomial mixing, Terminal A, or Terminal B.

## Target and convention

The project target remains the uniform law on fixed-length identity
transposition words.  M140 concerns the nonlazy stationary-flow
parking-degree projection.  Its convention is `(pq)(x)=p(q(x))`, with factors
displayed left-to-right.

1. Verify every supplied M140 SHA-256 value before reading the payload.
2. Re-derive the rooted-tree EGF first moment, Poisson majorant, beta integral,
   universal series bound, endpoint-mass ratio, uniform logarithmic estimate,
   rational correction, and final scalar constant.
3. Check that the exceptional reset subtracts exactly one power of `lambda`
   and that the `s=6` boundary is strict.
4. Build an independently structured exact checker under this directory and
   regenerate the 1,222 finite and 4,212 tail Bernstein coefficients, their
   minima, and canonical digests.
5. Issue an explicit ACCEPT or REJECT with numbered objections, a nine-field

## Expected failure modes

- an EGF index/end-point shift;
- reversed stochastic domination or a Poisson parameter exceeding one;
- a nonuniform or incorrectly signed logarithmic bound;
- loss of the `99/100` mass factor;
- reuse of the rejected two-power exceptional subtraction;
- boundary killing inconsistent with `s=6`;
- coefficient ordering or digest definitions that conceal a mismatch.

The checker uses Python 3 standard-library `fractions.Fraction` arithmetic and
no stochastic input (`seed=null`).
