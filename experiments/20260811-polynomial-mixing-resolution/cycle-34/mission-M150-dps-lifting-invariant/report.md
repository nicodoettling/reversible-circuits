## Field 3 — explicit cover and exact invariant

In the Euclidean Clifford algebra, put

```text
a_(ij)=(e_i-e_j)/sqrt(2).
```

Its twisted adjoint action is transposition `(ij)`, yielding the explicit Pin
extension

```text
1 -> {+/-1} -> S~_d -> S_d -> 1.
```

The coordinate product restricted over

```text
P_(d,s)={(g_1,...,g_s):sgn(g_1)=...=sgn(g_s)}
```

has kernel `Z=<z_1,...,z_s>`. All tuples of transpositions form one
`P_(d,s)`-conjugacy class: coordinate transporters can independently be made
even by multiplying an odd transporter by the source transposition.

For a class representative `x` and lift `x~`, quotient `Z` by the subgroup
`K_C` generated by lift commutators with the base centralizer. The resulting
lifted class maps bijectively to the base class. The product of these unique
lifts over a product-one factorization lies in `Z/K_C`.

Conjugacy equivariance gives

```text
ell(b^(-1)ab)=ell(b)^(-1)ell(a)ell(b),
```

so the lifted product is exactly preserved by both signed Hurwitz directions.
This is an exact necessary invariant. Equal values are not sufficient.

## Field 4 — full common-parity centralizer kills every coordinate

`v_q` disjoint from `t_q` and form the tuple `h^(q)` whose coordinate `q` is

If `a,b` are the root lifts of `t_q,v_q`, orthogonality gives `ab=-ba` and

```text

```


centralizer witness, which could kill only a diagonal kernel relation.
