## Outcome

This is a positive feasibility resolution with one deliberately retained
qualification.  The run proves a polynomial-time rank-recursive sampler for
the scheduled dense bridge whose total-variation error is a fixed negligible
function of `N`.  It does **not** give an exact sampler or a sampler meeting
every externally requested `epsilon`: at fixed `L=2N^3`, increasing the
runtime does not reduce the proved error below

```text
delta_N = 3N exp(-N/12288).
```

The objective explicitly asks for complexity polynomial in
`log(1/epsilon)`.  On the conservative literal reading that `epsilon` is an
arbitrary requested tolerance, the fixed floor prevents the stronger
classification.  R0 and R1 are fully audited, R2--R5 are materially solved,
and the remaining positive-connection lemma is strictly narrower than the
initial rank-recursion question, so the displayed classification is the
applicable one.

## Convention and target

Permutations are maps with

```text
(alpha beta)(x)=alpha(beta(x));
```

the rightmost displayed factor acts first.  Words are generated by successive
right multiplication.  Every sampler in the accepted chain returns a word of
the requested length with product exactly the requested endpoint.

The public target remains the unconditioned uniform law

```text
U_N = Uniform Omega(N,2N^3;e).
```

Arbitrary endpoints appear only as recursive states.  The exact feasibility
test used at every rank is

```text
Omega(r,L;g) nonempty
iff sign(g)=(-1)^L and L>=r-cycles(g).
```

The proofs and implementations cover `N=2`, `L=0`, odd lengths, minimal
endpoint length, and padding by adjacent transposition pairs.

## Strongest accepted theorem and algorithm

### Audited fixed-negligible dense rank sampler

For every sufficiently large `N` and every feasible `g in S_N`, there is a
Las Vegas algorithm `DenseSample(N,2N^3,g)` such that:

1. it always outputs exactly `2N^3` transpositions with displayed product
   `g`;
2. its endpoint state uses `O(N log N)` bits and its branch inventory has
   `O(L)` polynomial-bit integers;
3. its expected Turing runtime is polynomial in `N` and `L`, with the
   conservative bound `O(N^4 L^3)` exact-arithmetic operations on
   polynomial-bit integers; and
4. uniformly over feasible `g`,

   ```text
   d_TV(Law(DenseSample(N,2N^3,g)),
        Uniform Omega(N,2N^3;g))
      <= 3N exp(-N/12288).                            (A)
   ```

The explicit large-`N` inequalities are stated in
`roles/r4-r5-closure/report.md`, equation (6.1).  Ranks for which they fail
form a fixed finite set and can be handled by exact finite-state bridge DP,
giving a formal all-`N` algorithm with a large, impractical constant.  At the
public endpoint `g=e`, (A) is the requested dense identity-bridge result at
its fixed accuracy.

The positive recursion works as follows.

- R1 stable-sorts every word into old factors in `S_(r-1)` and star factors
  `(i r)`, with exact star endpoint `q=h^{-1}g`, then inverts the sort for an
  arbitrary binary interleaving.
- In an old-long branch, R2 flattens the long old endpoint walk pointwise,
  samples the exact star coset through a two-state token bridge, recovers the
  actual labeled `h=gq^{-1}`, and recurses.
- In a star-long branch, R2 draws the actual old word unconditionally and R3
  samples the prescribed star fiber exactly; this branch terminates the rank
  recursion.
- Global-top-rank floors `B_r` retain only old-long branches whose child
  length remains in the next dense domain.  The discarded mass is bounded
  under the endpoint-conditioned bridge, not under an unconditioned proxy.
- The exact telescope is

  ```text
  D_r <= exp(-N/12288)+2exp(-Nr/4)+D_(r-1),
  D_4=0,
  ```

  which sums to (A).

This construction supports every feasible length and endpoint that it
actually generates internally; it does not recurse only on lengths `2r^3`.

### Dense complete-support theorem

Independently, with `M=binom(N,2)`, R0 proves for `N>=3` and every even `L`

```text
Pr[not Full_N]
 <= M(1-1/M)^L + (M N!/2)(1-2/N)^L.                 (B)
```

At `L=2N^3`, (B) is at most `exp(-2N)`; for `N=2` the failure probability
is zero.  Thus conditioning `U_N` on complete transposition support changes
the law by exactly `Pr_U[not Full_N]`, but the sampler itself targets `U_N`
and carries no edge-coverage mask.

## Exact dependencies and unresolved assumptions

The accepted analytic chain uses two external theorems whose primary-source
statements, hypotheses, normalizations, and composition conventions were
checked during the run:

- Caputo--Liggett--Richthammer,
  [*Proof of Aldous' spectral gap conjecture*](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0894-0347-10-00659-4),
  Theorem 1.1.  It supplies equality of interchange and one-particle gaps on
  the deleted-edge complete graph, the complete graph, and the star graph.
  Sign twisting converts the periodic nonlazy walks to the required absolute
  nontrivial spectral bounds.
- Féray,
  [*Partial Jucys--Murphy elements and star factorizations*](https://arxiv.org/html/0904.4854v2),
  Proposition 1.1, Theorem 1.2, and Lemma 3.1.  They supply the arbitrary-length
  transitive star coefficient; the run separately proves the positive
  exact-used-support decomposition and the uniform backward sampler.

The other accepted ingredients are proved in the role reports: the R1
bijection, feasibility, the token recurrence, likelihood-ratio comparison,
bridge-conditioned binomial tail, recursion invariant, and TV telescope.
Exact weighted draws assume unbiased random bits and use rejection sampling.

No connected-support Hurwitz-transitivity theorem, local `{3,4,5}` mixing
hypothesis, or conjectural polynomial gap is used.  Finite enumeration is
only a falsification and translation audit; it is not evidence for the
asymptotic bounds.

The remaining mathematical limitation is not an unstated assumption: it is
the explicit fixed error floor in (A).  The finite-rank exact fallback is a
theoretical uniformity device and is not claimed practical.

## First standalone open lemma

```text
Tunable positive connection lemma.

For every feasible (r,L,g) and every 0<epsilon<1, sample the positive
labeled R1 branch law within epsilon in time polynomial in
r, L, and log(1/epsilon), without enumerating S_(r-1) or a
partition-scale type table; alternatively, give an exact polynomial-time
sampler for that branch law.
```

Solving this lemma would remove the only accuracy gap between the audited
fixed-negligible architecture and a fully epsilon-parameterized rank
recursion.  The natural labeled table has `(r-1)!` entries and the natural
pointed-type table has `sum_{u<r}p(u)=2^{Omega(sqrt r)}` states, but those are
obstructions only to those implementations, not hardness theorems.

## Architecture decision

Promote the rank recursion at the precise scope proved here: a positive,
polynomial-time, fixed-negligible-error architecture for the scheduled dense
identity bridge, with exact prescribed-star completion and arbitrary-endpoint
recursive closure.  Do not promote it as exact or as satisfying every
requested `epsilon` until the open lemma above is resolved.

Retain the R6 negative result as well: adjacent one-pair insertion followed
by uniform conjugation and any amount of Hurwitz postprocessing cannot be
exactly uniform, because its weighted support-component signature has the
wrong law.  This does not conflict with the accepted rank architecture,
which is not confined to those component masses.
