# R3 star-bridge lane plan

## Objective and target

Resolve the prescribed-endpoint star subproblem used inside the positive rank
disintegration.  For fixed `N >= 1`, let

```text
J_N = {(i N): 1 <= i < N}
S_N(k,q) = { (a_1,...,a_k) in J_N^k : a_1 ... a_k = q }.
```

The target distribution is the exact uniform law on `S_N(k,q)`, for every
feasible arbitrary endpoint `q` and length `k` generated by recursion.  The
word convention will be fixed after the source/convention audit and used
consistently in proof and code.

## Named claims to test

1. A sharp feasibility/minimal-length theorem for star words, including
   `N=2`, `k=0`, parity, minimal length, nontransitive support, and pair
   padding.
2. A positive exact dynamic program, compressed by the stabilizer
   `H=S_{N-1}`, that counts every endpoint fiber and backward-samples an
   actual labeled word uniformly.
3. A rigorous state-count and bit-complexity bound for that DP.  In
   particular, determine whether the natural orbit state is polynomial-size
   or partition-scale; any negative conclusion will be limited to the named
   implementation.
4. Whether a further positive recurrence yields a genuinely polynomial-time
   exact sampler, or else the first standalone unresolved compression lemma.

## Work plan

1. Lock inherited definitions, multiplication convention, and primary-source
   scope from the project note, sampling/experiment references, and the named
   exact-sampling audit.
2. Derive the stabilizer-orbit descriptor and transition multiplicities;
   prove positivity, orbit completeness, and labeled reconstruction.
3. Implement exact brute-force and orbit-DP counts, compare all endpoints at
   least through the required tiny cases, and test exact backward sampling.
4. Analyze state count, integer bit lengths, preprocessing and per-sample
   complexity; separate transitive/nontransitive and minimal/arbitrary length.
5. Write a named-claim checkpoint and a final proof report with claim labels,
   limitations, and reproducible machine-readable evidence.

## Experiment parameters

- Exhaustive coefficient/count comparisons for all endpoints wherever
  feasible, targeting at least `N <= 5`, `k <= 6`.
- Boundary fixtures: `N=1,2`; `k=0`; impossible parity; minimal star length;
  and pair-padded feasible lengths.
- Python exact integers only; fixed seeds for any sampling diagnostics.
- Outputs: script(s), JSON results, command/environment metadata, and report.

## Expected failure modes

- The full `H`-orbit descriptor may have partition-scale rather than
  polynomially many states.
- A character/content formula may count with cancellation or fail to give a
  positive labeled sampler.
- Transitive minimal star-factorization formulas may not cover arbitrary
  endpoints, disconnected support, or nonminimal lengths.
- A cycle-type compression may lose the distinguished orbit data needed to
  update under right multiplication by a star transposition.
- Exact integer counts can have large bit length; arithmetic cost must be
  included rather than treating integers as unit-cost.

results.  No project-level source-of-truth file will be edited.
