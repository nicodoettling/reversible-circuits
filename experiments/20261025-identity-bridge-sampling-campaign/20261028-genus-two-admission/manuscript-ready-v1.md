# Manuscript-ready scoped statements — I1

Integration text for later operator use; the campaign does not edit the manuscript. Exact proofs and review caveats are in reconciliation-v1.md. Same-model internal review is correlated.

For a specified permutation sigma on a fixed set of v labels and g in{0,1,2}, let F(v,sigma,g) be its connected ordered transposition factorizations of length v+c(sigma)−2+2g. There is an exact uniform sampler for every nonempty such fiber, using independent fair bits and expected O(v^11 log^3(v+1)) bit operations. The singleton empty fiber atg0 is handled directly and positive-genus singleton inputs are rejected. The proof combines the published genus-two fixed-permutation count with a positive constant-marker expansion, a polynomial conditional oracle for allocating individual endpoint cycles, and a complete final-transposition disintegration. In particular, it samples connected identity words of length2v+2. Degreev, rather than logv, is the input-size parameter. See roles/r1-recursive/proof-v1.md and roles/r2-structural/review-recursive-v1/report-v1.md.

A second construction samples the connected identity genus-two fiber exactly by marking a cut at which total prefix genus is one. Every target word has such a cut, and its incidence graph is either a decorated tree or a decorated unicycle. Exact local genus0/1 counts, label/interleaving multiplicities and a positive conditional moment parser produce a uniform marked pair. Acceptance with probability reciprocal to the number of valid cuts removes the mark with expected at most2v+1 proposals. A conservative bit bound is O(v^20 log^4(v+1)). This construction depends only on the inherited genus0/1 decoder and does not claim arbitrary-endpoint genus2 through the same grammar. See roles/r2-structural/proof-v1.md and coordinator/structural-review-v1.md.

These primitive improvements do not resolve global sampling. Let j>=64, N=2^(8j), m=2^(6j), and L=max{v<=N:3^v<=(N+m+2)^4}. For every integer0<=G<=2^j, the uniform identity-bridge probability that every component has genus at mostG or size at mostL is at most2^(−1−jm/2). Hence every sampler supported on that event has total-variation error at least1−2^(−1−jm/2). The bound follows from a uniform genus majorant, exact component assembly and a lower bound on the exact bridge count obtained from an embedded subgroup. It is an obstruction to this component policy on this subsequence, not a general sampling impossibility. See roles/r3-mass/proof-v1.md and roles/r1-recursive/review-mass-v1/report-v1.md. The unrestricted all-length polynomial-time sampling question remains open.
