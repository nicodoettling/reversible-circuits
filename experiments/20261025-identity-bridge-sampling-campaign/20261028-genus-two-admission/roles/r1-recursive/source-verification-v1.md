2. I.P.Goulden and D.M.Jackson, *A proof of a conjecture for the number of ramified coverings of the sphere by the torus*, [primary arXiv PDF math/9902009](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9902009), version1,1Feb1999. Read Section2 (PDF page2) and Section3 eq(2): μ=|Class_alpha|/v! times the number c_g(alpha) of ordered transitive transposition factorizations of a fixed permutation, for arbitrary g. The double-torus introduction explicitly retains this paper's conventions. This is the precise normalization source; a degree2 weighted cover is not mistaken for an unweighted equivalence-class count.

I.P.Goulden,D.M.Jackson,R.Vakil, *The Gromov–Witten potential of a point, Hurwitz numbers, and Hodge integrals*, revised22Dec2000, [primary author-hosted PDF](https://math.stanford.edu/~vakil/files/gjvfinal.pdf).
