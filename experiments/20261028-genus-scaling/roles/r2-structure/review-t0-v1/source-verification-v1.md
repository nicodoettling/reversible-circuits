NONBLIND exact audit,2026-10-28. Direct primary PDF text/formula inspection; screenshot requests did not yield a reliably inspectable rendering, so no visual-page verification is claimed.

[Goulden–Jackson–Vakil, revised22December2000](https://math.stanford.edu/~vakil/files/gjvfinal.pdf): checked Section2.1 notation, Section2.3 equation13 and Definition2.2, equations14–16, Theorem3.2 and its proof on printed11–12. The partition symbol in16 requires parts>=2. The theorem identifies the rational-series constants with signed primitive Hodge integrals; equation13 gives the polynomial in individual cycle lengths. Its degree follows from the dimension condition, with Hodge degree between0 and g. The denominator exponent and repeated-part factorials agree with the reviewed contract. Genus>=2 is stable for every positive number of marked cycles, so the interpolation points avoid genus-zero stability exceptions. These are imported published identities, not an algorithmic oracle or a new proof of their geometry.

[Goulden–Jackson, arXiv:math/9902009v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9902009): checked Section2, printed2, and Section3 equation2. Its fixed endpoint count multiplied by conjugacy-class size/N! is the covering number. Thus C/z_alpha is the covering normalization, and the series coefficient is C/(r!z_alpha). Transitive transpositions are connected support; reversal changes endpoint to inverse and preserves its cycle lengths. This verifies the convention translation used by both frozen proofs.

The algebra and algorithmic complexity beyond these imported identities are audited independently in report-v1.md. No broad literature review or additional theorem was substituted.
